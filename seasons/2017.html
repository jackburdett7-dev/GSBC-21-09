Season Archive

2017 Season

A historic year — three Guildford teams on the diamond for the first time. Millers reach the National Final.

Season Review

How the season unfolded

Guildford Mavericks

The Mavericks "found the going a bit tougher" in Double-A, finishing with 4 wins. Despite the record, the team finished the season positively — achieving a 2-game win streak to close out the campaign. Dave Ferguson won MVP.

Guildford Millers

The story of the season. The Millers reached the National Final — narrowly losing to unbeaten Norwich in a game that "took the full 9 innings, for the first time by any club that year." A remarkable achievement for the team. Tetsuro Shinkawa named MVP.

Gold Cats — Inaugural Season

The Gold Cats entered competitive play for the first time, winning 3 games in their debut Single-A season. Richard King named MVP of the inaugural campaign.

Club record: 16 players made their debuts in 2017 — the most in any single season in the club's history. Many came through as the Gold Cats added a third competitive roster.

2017 debuts: Joel Birks, Ola Bjekevoll, Myles Buckton, Ollie Edser, Dave Ferguson, Danny Gosling, Mario Hanzak, Matt Heydenrych, Hans Hilder, Tom Lunn, Ollie Morris, Jasper Neary, Carolina Padron, Luis Padron, Tom Sullivan, Sammy Walton.
