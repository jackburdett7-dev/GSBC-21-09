Season Archive

2019 Season

Mavericks finish strong to clinch the playoffs. Six junior graduates step up to senior baseball.

Season Review

How the season unfolded

Guildford Mavericks

A tale of two halves — the Mavericks won just 2 of their first 9 games, then turned it around completely to finish with a 3-game winning streak and secure a playoff spot. In the playoff game against Milton Keynes Bucks at Farnham Park, they fell short with several key players missing.

Six players progressed from the junior programme in 2019: Matt Taylor, Jimmy Shaw, Lewis Bawden, Matt Friend, Ollie Edser, and Jack Burdett. Matt Taylor hit a 3-run home run in the first inning of the playoff game. Yasu Minowa was "in a class of his own" on the mound.

Guildford Millers

A challenging year in BBF Single-A South Pool-B, finishing 6th of 7. The Millers demonstrated progression with a roster of new players and junior graduates, but the competition in the pool was stiff.

Chris O'Connell hit a home run in the final game at South Coast Pirates. MVP Mike Payne contributed both as pitcher and batter, including a home run in the season finale. First visit by a Guildford team to Bristol for away matches.
