Season Archive

2016 Season

The club's 25th anniversary — and the birth of the Guildford Millers. Both teams finish with winning records.

Season Review

How the season unfolded

Guildford Mavericks

The Mavericks commemorated the club's 25th anniversary — marking the first game in April 1992 — with a winning record in AA. It was also the first time in three seasons (since 2013) the club was able to field two teams. Chris Ward won MVP for the Mavericks, with Michael Garner leading the offence.

Guildford Millers — Inaugural Season

Named to honour the club's origin as the Wokingham Millers, the Millers launched their first season in Single-A managed by Ben Stobart and Will Frawley. They achieved a winning record and reached the playoffs in their very first competitive season — eliminated by eventual champions Tonbridge at Farnham Park.

Tetsuro Shinkawa won MVP. Lewis Bawden was named Rookie of the Year in his first senior season.
