Getting here

Kings College, Guildford.

Our home ground is at Kings College, Southway, Guildford GU2 8DU. Well served by public transport from Guildford town centre.

Park off Park Barn Way, at the back of the school, not at the main entrance. The diamond is on the sports field behind the school.
