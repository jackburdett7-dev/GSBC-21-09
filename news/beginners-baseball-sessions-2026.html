Beginners Baseball Sessions 2026

By Michael Burdett · 1 February 2026

Looking to try a new sport?

Perhaps wanting to get back into the game having played previosuly?

Maybe wanting something for the summer months?

Or simply want to expand your social circle and get to know more poeple locally?

Come and try baseball, with Guildford Baseball Club

We are looking for more players at all levels of experience and will be running sessions through February and March in the lead up to the new season in April.

They will be indoors to start with (at Broadwater School, Godalming) until the end of Feb, and will then venture outside, once the weather improves, at Kings College in Guildford.

There is no charge for new players who want to come along and try it out, before deciding whether to join. We can also supply all eqpt. So you just need clothing/footwear suitable for either indoors or outdoors.

These are open to men and woman, 16 and up (if you are under 16, we will also be running junior sessions shortly as well, so please contact us)

If you are interested in the indoor sessions, then you can book a place using the link below, or just come along on the day

Registration Form – Beginners Baseball

Dates as follows:

Sunday, Feb 8th – 2-4pm
Sunday, Feb 15th – 2-4pm
Sunday, Feb 22nd – 1-3 pm
Sunday, Mar 1st – 1-3 pm

(all sessions at Broadwater School Sports Centre)
