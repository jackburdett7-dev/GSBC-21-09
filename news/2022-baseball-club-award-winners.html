2022 Baseball Club Award Winners

By Michael Burdett · 12 November 2022

The club held is annaul awards event on Sunday 16th October. Another good turnout was on hand to recognise those who consistnely produced the best performances during the 2022 season. It was particularly pleasing to see 5 of the award winners for the adult teams, go to players who had progressed through the clubs junior programme. In addition to this, the volunteer aware went to Richard Williams who was absolutely key this season in ensuring the juior programme continued, especially in the very important U11 group.
